Contact Tracker Privacy Policy.

Effective November 2, 2026

What the Contact Tracker is.

The Contact Tracker is part of the Partner Resources site for OS Partners of The Operator Standard. It helps partners remember who they talked to about The Operator Standard, what they talked about, and when to follow up.

What you put in it.

Only what you type: names, how you met, dates, phone numbers, email addresses, social handles, notes, and whether someone joined or got a letter. Only add details people shared with you.

Where it's kept.

  • If you don't sign in: your list is saved in your browser on your phone. It is never sent to us.
  • If you sign in with Google: your list is saved in a spreadsheet called "OS Contact Tracker" in your own Google Drive. You own it. You can open it in Google Sheets, share it, or delete it like any other file.

What we can see.

Nothing. The Contact Tracker has no server and no database of its own. We can't see, collect, sell, or share your list.

What it does with your Google account.

When you sign in, the tracker asks for one permission: to create and edit files that it makes in your Google Drive. It uses that only to create, read, and update your "OS Contact Tracker" spreadsheet. It cannot see your other Drive files, your email, or anything else in your account.

The Contact Tracker's use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Sign-in lasts up to an hour. Google's sign-in pass is kept only in your browser on your phone until it runs out, so you can come back within the hour without signing in again. We never receive it, and nothing about your Google account is stored by us.

Turning it off.

  • Tap Sign Out in the tracker at any time.
  • Remove the tracker's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Delete the "OS Contact Tracker" spreadsheet from your Drive to delete your synced list.
  • Delete people in the tracker, or clear your browser's data, to remove the copy on your phone.

The rest of the Partner Resources site.

The Partner Resources site counts anonymous visits, video plays, and guide downloads so we can see what's useful. Those counts use a random ID stored in your browser. They don't include your name, your Google account, or anything you put in the Contact Tracker.

Changes and questions.

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top. Questions: matt@andyfrisella.com.
